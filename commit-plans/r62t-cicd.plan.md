Budget **25–45 Linux minutes for Jini** and **35–60 for Tovu**, excluding route coverage runs and release rehearsals. Record per-job billed time from two runs on the standard runner. At eight full runs in each repo, budget **480–840 minutes**, then add event runs, existing workflows and release runs from the billing dashboard. Use the account’s spending controls to keep paid overage at zero.
