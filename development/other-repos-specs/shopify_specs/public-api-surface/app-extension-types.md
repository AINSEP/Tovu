# Shopify App Extension Types

## Shopify Admin Extensions

1. **Admin Actions** - "Add custom modals to resource pages in the Shopify admin."

2. **Admin Blocks** - "Add custom cards to resource pages in the Shopify admin."

3. **Product Configuration** - Enables interaction with bundles on product details pages within the admin interface.

4. **Admin Link Extensions** - "Add quick links to your app from any page in the Shopify admin."

5. **Channel Config Extension** - Lets developers configure their app as a sales channel and establish channel specifications.

6. **Discount Function Settings** - Permits configuration of app discounts on the discount details page.

7. **Navigation Links** - "Add navigation links to display your app's navigation items consistently across devices."

8. **Purchase Options Extensions** - Introduces purchase options for users in the Shopify admin.

9. **Subscription Link** - Customizes links for viewing subscription details in subscription-enabled applications.

10. **Web Pixel (Admin)** - "Run JavaScript code snippets on the Order status page" for data collection.

## Checkout Extensions

11. **Checkout UI Extensions** - "Add custom workflows and functionality at defined points in the checkout process."

12. **Shopify Functions** - Injects custom code into key platform areas like checkout or cart.

13. **Post-Purchase** (requires approval) - Helps merchants increase sales through post-checkout product offers.

14. **Web Pixel (Checkout)** - Collects behavioral data on online stores for marketing optimization.

## Customer & Flow Extensions

15. **Customer Account UI Extensions** - "Add functionality at defined points in customer accounts."

16. **Flow Triggers** - Connects your app so events trigger workflows.

17. **Flow Actions** - Allows your app to receive data when workflow actions execute.

18. **Flow Templates** (requires approval) - Creates example workflows available in Flow's template library.

19. **Lifecycle Events** - Notifies your app when your triggers are actively used.

## Online Store & Payments Extensions

20. **Theme App Extensions** - Integrates with Online Store 2.0 themes.

21. **Payments Extension** (requires approval) - Enables customers to complete purchases using your payment method.

## Point of Sale

22. **POS UI Extensions** - "Add custom functionality at defined areas in the POS app."
