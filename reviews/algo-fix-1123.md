## OWNER-Q (decisions only the owner can make; recommendation first)

1. **P13 Q10: delete the unused `WorkspaceChatPane`.** Codex recommended this, and it reverses the plan's earlier "move". Choosing "move" brings in Q12 (split it) and Q13 (verified hook gate plus styles).
2. **P3-8: keep the run-status types additive, released as an `@jini-ai/daemon` minor.** The alternative is one widened `RoutineRunStatus` released as a major.
3. **P3-9: approve the Jini acceptance additions** (`findByMessage`, `tombstone`, no launch for a terminal run). Without them, the window after `starting` cannot be closed, and the routines feature stays hidden.
4. **P4 R1123-P4-1: approve the new public `ReportedFailure` carrier.** It replaces identity-based deduplication.
5. **P11 11a: restrict automatic byte removal.** It should only run for a confirmed purge or proven-disposable data until TTL eligibility is approved. This changes the earlier "eligible" recommendation.
6. **P11 OQ-3: gate destructive maintenance on proven exclusion of old binaries.** Seven days of time does not prove they are gone.
7. **P5 Q5: old binaries cannot be blocked.** Keep the version-compatible layout and keep cutover disabled until no supported old binary remains.

All other Codex recommendations are adopted as written. They are listed in the consolidated R1123 OWNER-Q section of each design (P3, P4, P5, P11) and in the R1123 note under P13 §5.
