How Fernbridge rebuilt their marketing pages faster with Meridian

Fernbridge is a leading financial infrastructure platform, powering payments and revenue operations for millions of businesses globally.

FoundedMarch 13, 2012
JoinedNovember 12, 2025

When Fernbridge was small, friction was manageable. A handful of engineers could coordinate in a single chat thread, ship a new page in an afternoon, and redesign entire sections of the marketing site over a weekend.

But scale changes the gravity of everything. As Fernbridge grew — more countries, more products, more markets — points of friction didn't simply add up; they multiplied. Before Meridian, teams built pages using slightly different layouts, custom patterns, and individually maintained components.

The change didn't start with a mandate. It began with one team experimenting with Meridian, looking for a faster way to launch a feature page without reinventing layouts. The fit was immediate — the momentum built naturally, driven not by policy, but by utility.

Fernbridge's marketing team now ships pages at a pace that matches the company's ambitions. Large product launches that once required weeks can be assembled in days.

"We needed a solution that could handle our complex UI requirements while maintaining performance. Meridian delivered exactly that."
