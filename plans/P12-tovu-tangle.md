**Start with the Tovu halves of the route adapter, AdminKit, and approval contract, then cut the admin transport and locale bindings.** The route adapter is a prerequisite for **334** files with K1 work. AdminKit 0a makes another **54** files eligible at the kit boundary. The transport and locale modules have **138** and **91** direct importers respectively. Those are counts of files that lose a prerequisite, with overlap. I estimate **about 500 plain moves out of the requested ~2,096**, with a modeled range of **360–580**.

The import census covers **2,313** production paths selected from the 2,443 `k-work.json` rows that exist at that ref. I excluded 130 paths under the theme and retained publish-UI rules, preserving the four mixed theme slices restored by P9F. Counts are **distinct Jini-bound importer files per row**. The TypeScript syntax scan includes type imports, re-exports, and literal lazy imports, and resolves relative, `#src`, `@/`, and Tovu package paths. Group totals overlap. I used `P9F-check.md` for lane ownership, `P9U-untangle-v2.md` and `P9U-check-v2.md` for admin cutovers, `blocked-files.md` for blocker classification, and all seven ex4 lane reports for execution evidence.
