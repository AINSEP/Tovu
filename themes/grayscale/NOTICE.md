The original is a Pug/Sass/Bootstrap one-page portfolio template, not a Liquid
theme. Rebuilt here as a Tovu `declarative`-tier theme: `templates/home.json`
and `templates/entry.json` are authored fresh against Tovu's core component
registry (`tovu/nav`, `tovu/hero`, `tovu/section`, `tovu/feature-grid`,
`tovu/entry-list`, `tovu/cta`, `tovu/footer`), reproducing the original's bold
dark-masthead/one-page structure and its color palette + fonts
(`--accent` teal `#64a19d`, `Varela Round` / `Nunito`, from the original
`_variables.scss`). No original Pug, Sass, or JS source is included.
