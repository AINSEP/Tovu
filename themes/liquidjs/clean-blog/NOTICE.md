Rebuilt as a Tovu `templated`-tier theme: the masthead/post-feed/post layouts
were translated from Jekyll `_layouts`/`_includes` into `templates/home.liquid`
and `templates/entry.liquid` against Tovu's own template contract (`posts`,
`post.title/slug/date/content`, `render_block`). Colors and fonts (`--accent`
teal `#0085A1`, `Lora`/`Open Sans`) are taken from the original theme's
`_variables.scss`. Demo images (`img/`) and the Bootstrap/Sass/JS build
pipeline are not included — only `theme.json`, `tokens.json`, `styles.css`,
and `templates/` are needed for Tovu to load this theme.
