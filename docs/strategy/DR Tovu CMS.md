## Contact-discovery playbook for verified public emails

This is designed to produce exactly what you asked for: **publicly listed emails where available**, and otherwise the best alternative path—*with proof*.

### Stepwise workflow

**Step one: Confirm the correct person and the correct firm page**  
Use firm sites first (primary source), then verified profiles (LinkedIn, X), then reputable podcasts/newsletters.

**Step two: Search the person’s public footprint for an email**  
Where emails most commonly appear (highest yield to lowest yield):
- Personal newsletter (Substack/beehiiv), “contact” page, blog footer
- Podcast guest pages and speaker bios
- Conference/event speaker pages
- GitHub profile bio (sometimes)
- Firm bio pages (sometimes)
- Press pages (media contact often reveals org email patterns)

**Step three: If no email is present, find the best “deck intake” path**  
Examples of valid alternatives:
- firm “submit your pitch” form
- a platform team email (public)
- a general inbound alias (public)
- assistant/EA route if listed publicly

**Step four: If—and only if—you can prove a pattern, infer an address**  
Inference is allowed only when you can point to multiple publicly listed examples that follow the same format. Your output must label it “Inferred (unverified)” and include the evidence set.

- `"<Full Name>" email`
- `"<Full Name>" contact`

### Evidence capture standard

For every email you collect, store:
- exact page URL + date accessed
- screenshot or archived copy if possible
- whether it is explicitly listed (Confirmed) or pattern-inferred (Inferred)
- the surrounding context (bio footer, contact page, etc.)

This matters because investor contact data changes quickly, and sending decks to wrong addresses harms deliverability and credibility.
