6. **Other packages' re-export shells** of these names are deleted, not repointed (owner rule): `ui/src/features/panel-kit/index.ts` (`buildAgentListHandles`) and `ui/src/features/mcp-ui/protocol.ts` (the 14 values + 5 types). Those rows are in the TSV with `new_spec = -`.
