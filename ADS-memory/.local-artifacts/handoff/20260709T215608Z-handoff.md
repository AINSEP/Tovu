## Decisions already made (do not relitigate)
- **Commerce is Tier-3 *for now*** (full access, first-party/trusted); becomes the reference **Tier-2**
  plugin later once the sandbox ships. No conflict with ADR-024 sequencing.
- **Tier-2 = fake the sandbox, don't build it.** Analyzer runs over the frozen ABI as-if-walled
  (worker/message channel). Do NOT build per-site process isolation — that's the expensive deferred rung.
- **Start Tier-3 as a THIN SLICE:** products → their own table → listed on the site. Nothing else.
  Exercises the two irreversible things (the plugin↔Tovu ABI contract + plugin-owned tables with
  snapshot-before-every-schema-change). Checkout/payments come *after* the slice proves the foundation.
- **Optional cheap pre-check:** a tiny throwaway Tier-3 plugin against the ABI first (~1hr) to feel
  whether the frozen async/serializable/no-live-objects/caps-by-handle contract is painful, before
  committing to the store slice. (This is the single most valuable early finding — the ABI is
  irreversible under ADR-005's semver promise once third parties exist.)
