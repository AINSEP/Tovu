What we believe

Three ideas that shape everything we build

Fast beats feature-complete

A site that launches in two days changes how often teams redesign. We'll cut a feature before we let the editor get slow.

Version history is a first-class action

Every site builder talks about publishing forward. We spend just as much design effort making it easy to go back.

Templates are for humans

If a designer can't find the section they want to edit in ten seconds, the template has already failed its job.

We're a small, remote team — and hiring

If shaving thirty seconds off a page load feels satisfying to you, we'd like to meet you.
