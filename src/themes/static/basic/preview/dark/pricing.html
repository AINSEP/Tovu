FAQ

Questions teams usually ask before switching

What counts as a published site?

Any site with a live URL, whether on a Basic subdomain or your own custom domain. Draft-only sites don't count against your plan.

Can I export the raw HTML/CSS?

Team and Enterprise plans support full code export, so a site you built in Basic can move to your own hosting whenever you want.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes — paying annually on the Team plan works out to two months free compared to monthly billing.

What happens if we go over our published-site limit?

On Starter, new publishes pause until you unpublish a site or upgrade. Team and Enterprise plans have no site cap.

Do you offer a nonprofit or student discount?

Yes, reach out to support once you're signed in and we'll get a discounted plan set up.
