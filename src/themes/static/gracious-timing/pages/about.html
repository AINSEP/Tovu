FAQs

Questions we hear a lot

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope, but most branding or website projects take between 2–4 weeks. Complex apps or platforms may take longer — we'll always give you a clear timeline upfront.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes — every project includes a post-launch window for fixes, and we offer ongoing retainers for teams that want continuous iteration.

What if I only need design or development, not both?

That's fine — most of our engagements start as design-only or dev-only, and expand once the first phase proves out.

Can you work with my internal team?

Regularly. We're used to embedding alongside in-house designers and engineers rather than replacing them.

Do you work with startups or only established companies?

Both — our plans are built to flex from an early-stage MVP up to a multi-team enterprise rebrand.
